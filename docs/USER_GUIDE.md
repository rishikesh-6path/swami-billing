# ShopLedger: guide for shop staff

ShopLedger makes your bills and keeps your accounts. It works without the internet. You can use the mouse, but everything can also be done from the keyboard, which is faster at the counter.

## Starting

1. Switch on the computer. ShopLedger opens by itself. If it does not, double-click the ShopLedger icon on the desktop.
2. Click your name, type your PIN and press Enter.
3. The home screen shows today's sales, cash in hand, money to collect from customers and items running low.

Press **F1** on the home screen at any time to see the list of keys.

## Making a sale (the most common job)

1. Press **F8**. The cursor is in the Customer box.
2. For a cash sale, just press **Enter**. For a named customer, type a few letters of the name and press Enter on the one you want.
3. Type the item code (the short number) and press **Enter**. The item is picked at once. If you type a name instead, a list appears: use the Up and Down keys and press Enter.
4. Type the quantity and press **Enter**. The price appears by itself; type a new price if this customer gets a different one. Press Enter to move on.
5. For the next item, keep pressing Enter until a new line opens, then repeat from step 3.
6. The total is shown on the right as you go. GST is worked out for you.
7. Type the amount received in the "Received now" box if the customer pays something now.
8. Press **F2** to save. The bill number appears at the top. If printing after saving is switched on, the print screen opens.

Useful keys while making a bill:

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| F2 | Save the bill |
| Esc | Leave the bill (it asks first if you have typed something) |
| F7 | Repeat the line above |
| F9 | Delete the line you are on |
| F12 | Copy the last bill of the same kind |
| Alt+H | Set this bill aside and start a fresh one (for example when another customer is waiting) |
| Alt+R | Bring a set-aside bill back |
| F4 | Pick a standard note |
| F5 | Show the list for the box you are in |
| F10 | Calculator |

If you make a mistake in a line, move to the box and type over it. You cannot save a bill with a problem: ShopLedger tells you what is wrong and where.

| Home tile | Stock Count | Writing in what you counted on the shelf |
| Home tile | Stock Journal | Changing stock between items |

## Printing a bill

Open the bill (see "Finding, changing and cancelling" below) and press **Ctrl+P**. You see exactly what will print. Choose A4 sheet or the narrow receipt roll, then press **Enter** to print. "Save as PDF" makes a file you can send to the customer. If nothing prints, check the printer is on, has paper and is connected, then try again.

## Finding, changing and cancelling a bill

- Press **D** on the home screen. Type part of the customer name, the bill number or a note, choose the bill and press Enter.
- If a customer has a credit limit and this bill would take them above it, the totals panel says so. Staff cannot save such a bill and are told to ask the owner; the owner is asked to confirm. Taking some money now (the Received box) keeps the amount owed within the limit.
- **Alt+N** copies a sale or purchase as a new bill with today's date, so a regular order can be made again with one change. Nothing is saved until you press F2.
- **Alt+B** changes a bill. The old bill is kept and marked as replaced, and a corrected bill is saved in its place. Nothing is ever erased.
- **Alt+C** cancels a bill. You must write a short reason. A cancelled bill stays on record but no longer counts in sales or stock.
- If the owner has closed the day, you cannot change bills of that day. Ask the owner.

## Customers, suppliers and items

- Home tiles **Items**, **Customers** and **Suppliers** list them. Type to search, press Enter to open.
- Press Enter on a customer or supplier to see their page: what they owe, how much of it is late, and their latest bills and payments. **L** shows the full ledger and **F2** changes their details. On a customer's page **F8** starts a sale and **F6** a receipt; on a supplier's page **F9** starts a purchase and **F5** a payment. The person is already filled in.
- On a bill, after you choose the customer and an item, the price that customer paid last time is shown under the price box. Press **F3** to use it; otherwise the list price stays.
- The owner can raise or lower many prices at once: on the Items list press **Alt+P**, choose the group, type the per cent, press **F2** to see every new price, then **F2** again to save. Bills already made are not touched.
- **F2** adds an item, **F3** adds a customer. Fill the boxes with Enter and save with **F2**.
- The item "code" (alias) is the short number you type when billing. Keep each code different.

## Reports

Press **R** for the list. Every report can be saved as a spreadsheet (**Ctrl+E**) or printed (**Ctrl+P**).

- **Day Summary** and **Day Book**: what happened today.
- **Stock Report**: what is on the shelf and what is running low. Tick **Counting sheet** to print a list with an empty box to write what you count; then make a Stock Count entry from it.
- **Outstanding**: who owes you and whom you owe, with how many days overdue.
- **Account Ledger**: every entry for one customer or account. Press **L**.
- **Item History**: every movement of one item. Press **G**.
- **Items to Order**: items below their minimum stock, with how much is needed and who you last bought them from, at what price and when.

The owner also sees Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance and the GST reports, including **Purchases for the CA**: every supplier invoice by GST rate, saved as a spreadsheet for your accountant.

## If something goes wrong

- Read the message on the screen. It says what happened and what to do.
- If a bill was not saved, nothing was changed. Try again.
- If the screen asks you to call support, write down the message. Do not enter new bills until it is sorted out.
- Never switch off the computer by the power button while a bill is being saved. Always wait for "Saved".
